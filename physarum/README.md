# Slime mould finds the network

Millions of particles, each doing nothing cleverer than steering towards the strongest smell in front of it and leaving a trail of its own. Out of that comes a transport network - the same behaviour that lets Physarum polycephalum reproduce a country's rail map when food is placed at the cities.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is the whole of it - one file, no build,
no server, no keys. It is written for a 4K screen and scales down.

Press `i` for the parameters it is running, and the arrows appear if you move
a pointer.

## What it is doing

- There is no planner and no global view. Every structure on screen is the product of one local rule applied a few million times a second.
