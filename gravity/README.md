# An n-body system, integrated honestly

Bodies pulling on each other under Newtonian gravity, integrated forward with no scripted orbits and no choreography. What you see is what the equations do.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is the whole of it - one file, no build,
no server, no keys. It is written for a 4K screen and scales down.

Press `i` for the parameters it is running, and the arrows appear if you move
a pointer.

## What it is doing

- Watch for a body whose spin axis lies across your line of sight: it will visibly slow, stop and reverse as the camera works round it. That is correct projection geometry for a flat silhouette, not a bug.
