# A day of sun and wind over real ground

Eighteen hours behind and eighteen ahead, over one of three regions. The land is veiled in grey and sunlight lifts the veil, so the map shows through exactly where the sun is reaching the ground. The streaks are surface wind, drawn by letting tracers ride the field.

## Running it

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is the whole of it - one file, no build,
no server, no keys. It is written for a 4K screen and scales down.

Pin a region with <code>?loc=</code>: colorado, chesapeake, salish.

Press `i` for the parameters it is running, and the arrows appear if you move
a pointer.

## What it is doing

- The forecast is fetched live from Open-Meteo at a 7x8 grid of points and interpolated between them, so this one page does need a network.
- The base maps are satellite imagery cut to each region's bounds at build time; state lines and coastline are embedded in the page so they survive a dropped connection.
